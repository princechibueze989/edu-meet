Some quick details about the Edu-Meet platform.

What is Edu-Meet?+

Edu-Meet is a school management platform concept designed to bring school administration, student information, teacher coordination and attendance into one connected experience.

Who is Edu-Meet designed for?+

Its intended users include school administrators, teachers and other members of a school's community, depending on the features enabled by each school.

Can I use Edu-Meet on a phone?+

This landing page is responsive and designed to work on mobile, tablet and desktop screens. Dedicated app availability depends on publication in the relevant app stores.

Are the dashboard numbers real?+

No. The figures shown on this landing page are illustrative design examples, not live data from a school account.

How can my school get started?+

Use the contact link above to enquire about Edu-Meet. A live registration system will need to be connected separately if it is not already available.
